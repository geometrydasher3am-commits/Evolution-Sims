wolves stalk the flock — they run 10% faster than sheep, kill exactly one sheep in their lives, and only then become fertile: a touch alone never makes pups. after mating a wolf rests before seeking again. sheep panic and flee when a wolf gets close — cornered ones at the fence die.

every birth rolls a litter — 25% twins · 10% triplets · 5% quadruplets — for sheep and wolves alike. littermates are cross-linked: click one to see who it was born alongside. names are forged from syllables — a random start and a random end (sheep: Baa, Fluff, Kay, Mee… · wolves: Bol, Bor, Lov, Int…).

lambs inherit the exact blend of their parents' fleece — red × blue truly makes violet, yellow × blue makes green — and darkness is part of the color. each lamb can still mutate: 10% toward a primary tint, 10% darker or lighter.

lambs mature at 5 s · lifespan 60 s (wolves 45 s — the prey now outlives the predator) · births brake near 300. sheep that go a few seconds without touching anyone seek a mate at any flock size; under 7 the whole flock panics. lonely virgins single out elders first, so two virgins never waste their one chance.
